Read this before you use the email column

Two kinds of address appear here and they are not interchangeable. A recruitment inbox (Email) came from the firm’s own careers page which you should write to directly. A switchboard address (Firm contact) came from Chambers and is the firm’s general enquiries line.

The Legal 500 publishes no addresses at all. Adding Chambers is what makes this column usable: 110 firms now have some address. Where neither exists the firm takes applications through a portal or a candidature spontanée form.
